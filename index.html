About Dajai Studio

Dajai Studio is an independent game studio focused on creating original games and interactive experiences.

We believe great games are born from bold ideas, relentless iteration, and a deep respect for the player. Every pixel, every mechanic, every moment is crafted with intention.

From fast-paced arcade action to brain-teasing trivia, we build worlds that invite you to play, explore, and return.
